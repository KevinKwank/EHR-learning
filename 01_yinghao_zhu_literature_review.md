
# Yinghao Zhu related EHR research review

## Research trajectory

The researcher works around:
- structured longitudinal EHR prediction;
- EHR benchmarking;
- LLMs for clinical prediction;
- reproducible EHR evaluation.

Representative works identified:
1. Prompting Large Language Models for Zero-Shot Clinical Prediction with Structured Longitudinal Electronic Health Record Data (2024)
2. A Comprehensive Benchmark for COVID-19 Predictive Modeling Using Electronic Health Records in Intensive Care
3. ClinicRealm: Re-evaluating Large Language Models with Conventional Machine Learning for Non-Generative Clinical Prediction Tasks

## Relevance to our project

These works establish:
- how to benchmark EHR prediction tasks;
- how LLMs can consume structured longitudinal EHR;
- the gap between general LLM reasoning and temporal clinical understanding.

The remaining gap:
LLMs can process EHR sequences, but understanding "which past evidence matters at the current clinical moment" remains insufficient.
