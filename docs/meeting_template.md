## Presentation Tips

1. **Time management**: Aim for 15-20 minutes + 10 minutes discussion
2. **Figures**: Use the paper's original figures when possible; create simplified versions for clarity
3. **Equations**: Show 1-2 key equations max; explain intuition, not just math
4. **Connection**: Always end with how this relates to our research
5. **Interaction**: Ask the audience questions throughout, not just at the end
